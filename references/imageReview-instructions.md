# Project V3 imageReview instructions

Review the actual image against its expression, production brief and actual prior images. actualDimensions comes from the real file decoder: never infer pixel dimensions from a scaled preview. Photo typography MUST be not_applicable; check unwanted promotional text and preserve original product printing. Other modes need a typography boolean: check exact words, spelling, mobile legibility and integration. Never fail a deliberate photo for missing a title. Compare actual placement, crop, distance, scale, negative space, information and reading path. Keep coherent color/light and inspect detail edges for visible stripes/gutters. Each failure needs a visible location and actionable repair. Do not turn uncertainty into visible copy.
COMMERCIAL EXECUTION REVIEW:
SOURCE RECONCILIATION: Inspect original product pixels independently BEFORE reading the suggested narrative. Descriptions and previous summaries may contain mistakes. A claim copied from description is source=user, never source=image just because an image ID exists. If text contradicts a directly visible physical feature, preserve the photographed feature and keep the disputed claim out of the campaign; ask only if that conflict is critical. A still photo of an extended part does not prove folding/retraction, removability or an operating sequence. Require explicit reliable evidence of the mechanism before planning a transformed state. Never invent a hinge or altered plug shape to satisfy a storyline. Reviewers must recheck source provenance and visible physical structure before typography/composition, even when the plan labels a claim verified.
Check whether the production brief's visualProof is actually legible, the product is physically grounded, material/light has controlled detail, the focal hierarchy matches layoutBlueprint, and planned supporting modules carry useful evidence instead of clutter. A correct SKU floating over an empty gradient is insufficient when an action, context or evidence module was planned. Do not pass an image solely because its words are spelled correctly.
Reject camera/analysis narration in visible copy, including "手边的相对尺寸", "从上方看圆形盖面" and "回看外观". Preserve category content jobs: the final parameter table, any real supplied size chart, and apparel on-body imagery cannot be replaced by an appearance recap or generic portrait. Check labels, values, units and table row relationships against productInformation.
For continuous DETAIL pages, compare the actual previous bottom and current top across the width. Actual saved neighbor pixels take precedence over planned HEX seam targets. A matching plan color does not pass when the actual neighboring edge differs; conversely do not fail a visually continuous join just because both images differ from a planned color. Judge natural color/luminance/texture flow, not a flat colored strip.
Compare actual neighboring frames: can the buyer learn something different, and do shot distance, subject coverage, action and reading path change? Inspect text at 375px, reduce oversized generic slogans, and preserve useful subcopy/labels. Do not remove useful information just to make everything photographic. A deliberately wordless scene can pass without titles. Every failed judgment must identify a visible defect and a local repair; no subjective paid retry for a merely different but successful composition. Do not demand unsupported claims, props sold as accessories, invented dimensions or hidden mechanisms.
TYPOGRAPHY REVIEW: Read at a 375px-wide preview and check the visible reading order, not merely spelling. Flag concrete defects: competing display headlines, an oversized full-sentence heading, supporting copy too small to read, arbitrary Chinese line breaks/orphans, inconsistent alignment, tight group spacing, poor text/background contrast, annotations detached from their proof, or parameter rows styled as giant slogans. A matching generic font does not make a composition pass. Name the affected block and a local change in width, lines, weight, size or position; preserve all verified values and user-required wording. Small differences from planned coordinates are acceptable when the hierarchy works; aesthetic preference alone is not a defect.
In storyboard review classify an actual layout/legibility defect as kind=typography and submit the affected full section with revised textBlocks/typesetting or typography/layout fields. A no-op patch cannot resolve it. When copy changes, update typesetting.lines and supporting-module indices together. Avoid curing a weak layout by deleting all text or converting every frame to photography. In image review mark typography=false for actual hierarchy/readability failures, even if every character is correct; pure photography stays not_applicable.
PRODUCT VIEW BOUNDARY: Preserve product identity and construction, not the source camera pose. Actively plan multiple photographic viewpoints: frontal identity, three-quarter depth, side silhouette, overhead arrangement and close detail. A three-quarter view is a real oblique camera view, not merely an in-plane rotation or a compulsory near-frontal shot. Change camera height, azimuth and distance purposefully. Plausible perspective extrapolation of the existing exterior is allowed; it is illustrative photography, not evidence of unseen features.
Use supplied multi-view photos when available. With a single photo, preserve recognizable proportions, labels, visible controls and garment construction while allowing dimensional views, natural drape and staging. Do not add invented rear prints, ports, switches, seams, inner layers, mechanisms or accessories. If a requested angle would require a specific unknown feature, choose a different oblique/overhead crop or keep that feature out of view; retain the frame and its task. Rear feature studies and cutaways require actual supporting material. Never replace all shots with the same front pose.
Review actual contradictions or an explicit fabricated feature, not an angle term alone. "Might reveal an unseen side" is not a factual defect. Modest camera deviation is not a failed composition if the intended information, dimensionality and focal hierarchy remain effective.
